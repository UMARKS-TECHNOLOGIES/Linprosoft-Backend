# 📄 Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## **Linkprosoft**

**By Umarks Technologies**

---

## **1.0 Product Overview**

### **1.1 Product Description**

Linkprosoft is a web-based platform designed to connect individuals with everyday service needs to verified skilled professionals across Nigeria and beyond.

The platform focuses on:

* Location-based skill discovery
* Professional credibility and reviews
* Efficient matching between demand (users) and supply (service providers)

---

## **1.2 Problem Statement**

### **Key Problems Identified**

* Fragmented and unorganized skilled labor market
* Difficulty finding trusted professionals nearby
* Inefficient and outdated job-seeking methods
* Lack of professionalism and accountability
* Privacy and trust concerns

---

## **1.3 Target Users**

### **Primary Users**

* Individuals with daily service needs (repairs, home services, etc.)
* Skilled professionals seeking job opportunities
* People searching for trusted service providers

### **Secondary Users**

* SMEs needing freelance/contract workers
* Agencies managing skilled workers

---

## **1.4 Value Proposition**

Linkprosoft provides:

* Fast access to nearby professionals
* Verified profiles with ratings and reviews
* A structured digital marketplace for skills
* Increased visibility and income opportunities for professionals

---

## **1.5 Product Goals**

* Build a centralized marketplace for skilled services
* Improve trust through verification and ratings
* Enable fast and accurate skill-to-user matching
* Digitize and modernize job discovery

---

## **1.6 Revenue Model**

* Service commission fees
* Featured listings / promoted profiles
* Subscription plans for professionals
* Ads and partnerships

---

# **2.0 Functional Requirements**

## **2.1 Core Features**

### **User Features**

* User registration & login
* Search for professionals (location + skill-based)
* View profiles, ratings, and reviews
* Book or contact professionals
* Leave ratings and feedback

### **Professional Features**

* Profile creation (skills, experience, location)
* Portfolio uploads
* Availability status
* Job request notifications
* Ratings & review system

### **Admin Features**

* User and professional management
* Content moderation
* Dispute resolution
* Analytics dashboard

---

## **2.2 Matching System**

* Location-based matching (GPS or input location)
* Skill/category filtering
* Ranking based on:

  * Ratings
  * Proximity
  * Availability

---

## **2.3 Non-Functional Requirements**

* **Performance:** Fast search and response times
* **Scalability:** Support large user base
* **Security:** Data encryption, secure authentication
* **Reliability:** 99% uptime target
* **Usability:** Mobile-friendly and intuitive UI

---

# **3.0 User Flow**

### **Basic Flow**

1. User signs up / logs in
2. Searches for a service (e.g., plumber)
3. System shows nearby professionals
4. User views profiles and selects one
5. User contacts or books service
6. Service is completed
7. User leaves review
